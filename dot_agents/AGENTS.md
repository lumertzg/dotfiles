## Communication

- Keep responses focused, brief, and concise. Keep disclaimers and caveats short, and spend most of the response on the main answer.
- When asked to explain something, give a high-level summary unless an in-depth explanation is specifically requested.

## Coding

- Never commit or push unless asked. Leave the diff ready for review.
- Before opening a pull request, ask the user to review and approve its title and body.
- Comment only to clarify intent, contracts, invariants, or hidden constraints. Never narrate the code. Keep comments brief and current.
- Never use semicolons in documentation, descriptions, or comments.
